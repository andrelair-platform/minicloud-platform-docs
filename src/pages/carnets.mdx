## How one case travels

One deep write-up per subject is enough. The blog hosts the article, [System Design](/system-design) lets
you discover it by question, and the docs hold the reference diagram. The same case is then retold per
channel:

| Format | What I share |
|---|---|
| **Blog** | The full reasoning — what I'm protecting, why a local copy, what an off-site copy adds, and the restores I actually tested. |
| **Newsletter** | The story. *"If the machines running my apps become unreachable, can I still recover their data?"* — then how that question drove the choice. |
| **LinkedIn** | A question and one idea. *"If all my machines go dark, where is my backup?"* — then the criterion and a link to the case. |
| **Out loud** | *"I want to recover my data even if I lose the machines running my apps. An off-site copy covers that. Then I have to prove I can actually restore it."* |

For a hybrid choice, I always name **what I operate myself, what I delegate, and why** — those concrete
responsibilities are what give meaning to *open source*, *self-hosted* and *managed cloud service*.
